Biomass should be used as equally as coal.

It is already a known fact that fossil fuels, a nonrenewable energy source humanity is extremely dependant on, will not serve us forever. What sparks debates all over the planet is exactly how to address the issue, and how to prepare when all fossil fuels run out. While some say that we should immediately spend billions on renewable energy sources like wind and solar power, others believe that biomass, or organic matter, should be combusted in equal measure of fossil fuels, since it would not take too much technology and money, it would make fossil fuels last longer, and because biomass releases less emissions into the atmosphere.
